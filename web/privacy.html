1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of the personal data processed on Volo Index (voloindex.org) is the Give Protocol Foundation ("we", "us"), established in the United States.

Our representative in the EU and UK under Article 27 GDPR / UK GDPR is DataRep. You can contact DataRep in relation to Volo Index in any EU/UK member state through datarep.com/data-request.

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@voloindex.org.

4. Sensitive information in your interview answers

The assessment interview asks about your volunteering and leadership experience. It never asks for special-category data (such as health, religious, political, or trade-union information), and we ask you not to include such information in your answers — it is not needed for scoring.

If you do include something you'd rather remove, you can ask us to redact it from your stored interview at any time by contacting privacy@voloindex.org.

5. Publishing your certificate — your choice

By default your score, tier, and certificate are private — visible only to you. Your certificate is published at a public credential URL only if you give a separate, explicit opt-in. The public page shows only the certificate holder name, overall score and tier, and the score in each of the six competency dimensions.

You can withdraw that consent at any time. When you do, the certificate is removed from public access. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of publication before the withdrawal.

6. Automated scoring

Your evaluation score and tier are produced by automated means: an AI model conducts the interview and your responses are scored against our published rubric (competency signals across six dimensions, with defined band thresholds). The logic is documented in the scoring rubric available on this site.

The score exists to give you a credential; it has no effect beyond your account unless you opt in to publication, and every publication passes a human review step before going live. You have the right to request human review of your result, to express your point of view, and to contest a score — contact privacy@voloindex.org.

8. International data transfers

Our target posture is EU data residency for the primary data store. Where a provider processes data in the United States (Anthropic, Resend, Cloudflare, and any US-region infrastructure), the transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and/or the provider's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, supported by a transfer impact assessment.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR / UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate data;
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to the purchase-record retention above;
  • Receive your data in a portable format;
  • Restrict or object to processing;
  • Withdraw consent to certificate publication at any time (section 5);
  • Request human review of an automated score (section 6).

To exercise any of these, email privacy@voloindex.org. We respond within 30 days (Art. 12(3) GDPR).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority (in the UK, the ICO). You can reach an authority in your member state via our Article 27 representative, DataRep (section 1).

11. Age requirement

Volo Index is for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly process the personal data of anyone under 16. If you believe an account belongs to someone under 16, contact privacy@voloindex.org and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this notice

If we materially change what we collect, why, or who receives it, we will update this notice and change the date at the top; for significant changes we will notify account holders by email before the change takes effect.
